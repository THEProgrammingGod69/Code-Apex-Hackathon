Kaise Khelein

Phone

  • Left thumb anywhere on the left: move. Push up on a ladder to climb.
  • FIRE: hold to shoot. JUMP: tap or hold.
  • ★: special move (3 per bhai). THAPPAD: slap. It also bats bullets back.
  • Tip: Fullscreen from the menu, or Add to Home Screen.

Keyboard · Gamepad

  • AD / ←→ move · WS ladders
  • Space jump · J fire · L special · F thappad
  • Esc pause · Gamepad: A jump, X fire, B special, Y thappad

Bhaiforce rules

  • Push into a wall and hold JUMP to climb it, Broforce style.
  • Free caged bhais: +1 life and you instantly become that bhai, with fresh specials.
  • Raise the Tiranga at checkpoints. That's where the next bhai parachutes in.
  • Everything breaks. Red LPG cylinders go boom. Combos fill the score.
  • Beat the boss, then grab the Jugaad-copter's rope ladder to finish.
